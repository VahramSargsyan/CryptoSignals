## Frozen overlay candidates

### P1_ORIGINAL

- arm after +100% monthly surge versus prior completed selected-month extreme
- track post-arm running peak
- sell 30% after >=5% daily-close pullback from that peak
- execute next open
- natural re-entry at -25% from the cash-out locked peak
- execute next open
- 0.1% cash-out and re-entry costs

### P1_TRAILING_REENTRY

Same P1 cash-out rule.

While cash is parked:
- re-entry peak starts at the cash-out locked peak
- update that peak upward whenever reference daily-close equity makes a new high
- re-enter after a -25% daily-close pullback from the latest re-entry peak
- execute next open

No timeout.

### Secondary controls

P2_ORIGINAL and P2_TRAILING_REENTRY use the same rules except cash-out requires a -10% pullback rather than -5%.

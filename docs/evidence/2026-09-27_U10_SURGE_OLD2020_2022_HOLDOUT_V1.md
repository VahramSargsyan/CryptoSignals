## Main validation result

The old period separates two claims that looked connected in 2023-2026.

### Claim 1: large monthly U10-style surges are often followed by substantial pullbacks

SUPPORTED in this OLD10 holdout.

All 3 old-period +100% surge events reached at least -25% running drawdown within 31 days.

### Claim 2: the frozen P1/P2 trailing overlay can reliably monetize that effect

NOT SUPPORTED by this OLD10 holdout.

Both frozen overlays slightly underperformed the ordinary OLD10 baseline.

The drawdown benefit was very small.

Therefore:

> The descriptive surge/pullback effect appears temporally portable, but the current partial-cash/re-entry execution rule has not demonstrated a portable performance edge.

This is a meaningful negative validation and must not be tuned away using the 2020-2022 data.

## Interpretation

Why can the pattern be real while the overlay loses?

Because identifying a future pullback is not sufficient by itself.

Profit depends on:
- where the 30% cash-out is actually executed after the signal;
- whether the reference peak rises after cash-out;
- the next-open price at re-entry;
- the opportunity cost of being 30% out during any interim upside;
- fees;
- the interaction with U10's own asset rotations.

In 2021-01 and 2021-08, the reference portfolio made higher peaks after cash-out.

The trailing logic correctly moved the -25% threshold upward, but the net timing advantage still did not exceed the baseline path.

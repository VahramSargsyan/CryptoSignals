## Comparison with 2023-2026 discovery period

### Direct effect

2023-2026 alternative U10 topologies:
- -25% within 31d: 88.86%
- within 62d: 88.95%

2020-2022 old U10-like topologies:
- within 31d: 88.26%
- within 62d: 99.58%

The 31-day hit rate is remarkably similar across the two temporal periods.

That is the strongest evidence in the research so far that the underlying surge/pullback phenomenon is not only a 2023-2026 artifact.

### P1_ORIGINAL terminal benefit

2023-2026 alternatives:
- final > baseline: 96.21%
- median terminal delta: +23.44%

2020-2022:
- final > baseline: 88.64%
- median terminal delta: +12.36%

The direction replicates, but effect size and consistency are materially lower in the old period.

### Risk reduction

The overlay's drawdown benefit is much less robust in 2020-2022.

P1_ORIGINAL:
- only 60.45% had improved max DD
- only 49.09% improved both final equity and DD
- median DD improvement only +0.86 pp

Therefore the old validation supports the profit-lock's return effect more strongly than its drawdown-control effect.

### Trailing completed-cycle fallback

The trailing fallback does not validate strongly enough to call it a superior completion rule.

It solves unfinished cash cycles:
- 0% unfinished

But compared with the original frozen-peak rule:
- terminal benefit is less consistent
- drawdown improvement is weaker
- about 39% of P1 trailing old universes finish below baseline

Thus cycle completion has a measurable opportunity cost.

## Main conclusion

The historical validation separates two questions that should not be conflated.

### 1. Does the +100% surge -> large pullback phenomenon deserve attention?

YES, historically.

It replicated across:
- a different non-overlapping period
- different token compositions
- 220 exhaustive old U10-like universes
- 477 surge events

The 31-day -25% hit rate remained about 88% in both old and recent topology tests.

### 2. Have we solved the perfect cash re-entry rule?

NO.

P1_ORIGINAL has the strongest historical terminal evidence so far but can leave cash parked for long periods.

P1_TRAILING guarantees cycle completion but gives up too much of the original overlay advantage in the old validation.

Therefore do not tune another fallback using the now-open 2020-2022 data.

The correct scientific position is:

- the surge/pullback signal is validated strongly enough for continued study;
- P1_ORIGINAL has meaningful cross-period return evidence;
- the fallback/forced-completion mechanism remains unresolved;
- future paper/live observation is more valuable than further retrospective parameter hunting.

## Important 65-day observation

The old event study shows why a fixed 65-day intuition looked plausible:

July 2020:
- 0% of events hit -25% within 31 days
- 98.15% hit -25% within 62 days

But the 2025 regime already showed that 65d forced re-entry too early whenever a slower natural -25% correction eventually arrived.

Therefore the same number can look excellent in one cycle and fail in another.

This reinforces the decision not to optimize fixed timeout values.

## Guardrail

No live/paper behavior changed.

No further return-rule tuning should use the 2020-2022 outcomes.
